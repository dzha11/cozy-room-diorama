## Models and textures

All 3D objects are built in code from our own primitives (`src/Primitives.cpp`, `src/SceneLayout.cpp`).
All textures in `assets/textures/` (floor, wallpaper, wood, fabric, blanket, rug, poster, clock face, vinyl,
laptop screen, book spine) are original and generated by our script `tools/gen_textures.py`. No third-party
models or textures are used.
